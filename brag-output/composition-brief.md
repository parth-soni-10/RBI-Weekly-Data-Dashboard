# Composition Brief — RBI Weekly Dashboard

## Story arc
The Reserve Bank of India publishes a dense weekly spreadsheet. This dashboard turns it into a clean story: reserves, gold, rupee, markets — with 35 weeks of history.

## Visual language
- **Palette:** mint cream `#f4fdf7` scenes alternating with deep green `#0d2b1a` ink scenes
- **Accent:** `#16a34a` (the site's green), glow for emphasis
- **Type:** Inter (site font), heavy weights for numerals, tabular figures for counters
- **Motifs:** ₹ mark, KPI rows, bar chart of weekly reserves

## Scenes (30s, soft crossfades between scenes)

1. **Hook (0–7s)** — mint. Eyebrow "RBI WEEKLY DASHBOARD" spaced caps; headline "THE WEEK" + "IN NUMBERS" wordmark with ₹ mark.
2. **Proof (6.2–13.8s)** — mint. "Every Friday, the RBI drops a spreadsheet. Forex. Gold. Rupee. Markets. Buried in rows." Pills rise in on the beat grid: FOREX · GOLD · RUPEE · MARKETS.
3. **The Work (13–23s)** — ink. Dashboard card: "RBI Weekly Dashboard · Week of 21 Aug" with KPI rows (Forex Reserves $729B, Gold Holdings $114B, USD/INR 95.8) and a weekly-bar chart.
4. **Philosophy (22.2–27s)** — mint. Big quote mark + "Numbers move markets. Know where they're heading."
5. **Outro (26.2–30s)** — ink. "RBI WEEKLY DASHBOARD" + "The week, at a glance." + "India · Every Friday".
