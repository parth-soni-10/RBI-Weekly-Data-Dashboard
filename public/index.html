India's weekly economic data, with dates and sources

The Reserve Bank of India's Weekly Statistical Supplement (WSS) is a weekly release of monetary and banking statistics. This independent dashboard presents a history of reported foreign-exchange reserves and related weekly observations, with the source date kept distinct from the publication date.

Explore reserve totals and gold holdings, weekly rupee and market observations, and separately sourced macroeconomic context. The crude-oil section combines monthly import information from the Petroleum Planning & Analysis Cell (PPAC) with clearly identified vessel-tracking estimates; those estimates are not official monthly import totals. Each series should be interpreted using its displayed unit, date basis, and source.

    RBI forward book: net position (USD bn)

    How to read this
    Outstanding net forward contracts. Above zero = net forward assets (RBI buying USD forward); below = net forward liabilities (RBI selling USD forward). Month-end figures from the RBI Bulletin's forward-maturity table, cross-checked to RBI's half-yearly FX reserves reports (the two match). Each weekly view steps a released figure across weeks until the next one lands - flat stretches mean no newer disclosure yet. RBI publishes no currency split, so this is the aggregate USD figure. The since-2021 view draws a dashed zero baseline and flags the Apr-2024 week the book turned net short for good.
