Crude glossary: BPD = Barrels Per Day (daily import volume estimate). DWT = Deadweight Tonnage (vessel carrying capacity in metric tons; 1 DWT crude oil ≈ 7.33 barrels). AIS = Automatic Identification System (for live tanker tracking/position). PPAC = Petroleum Planning & Analysis Cell (official India monthly crude import stats). Brent/WTI = Benchmark crude prices (USD/bbl, fetched live via Yahoo Finance).

Notes & caveats: Figures are ₹ crore and reflect the audited Receipt & Payment Accounts published by the Fund on pmcares.gov.in (FY 2019-20 through FY 2022-23 released in Dec 2024; FY 2023-24 & FY 2024-25 released 18 Aug 2026). Interest (yr) = interest earned during the financial year - explicitly reported as ₹224.33 cr (FY 2020-21), ₹170.38 cr (FY 2022-23, of which ₹154 cr regular + ₹16.07 cr foreign account), and ₹475.14 cr (FY 2024-25, ₹469.37 cr fixed deposits + ₹5.76 cr regular account); ~nil in FY 2019-20 (7-day period). FY 2021-22 (~₹170.29 cr) is derived as the residual of the reported 4-year cumulative interest of ~₹565 cr (through FY 2022-23), and FY 2023-24 (~₹137.70 cr) is derived from receipts minus donations minus refunds - treat these two as estimates. Cumulative contributions received since inception (~₹13,605 cr) and cumulative interest (~₹565 cr) were reported through FY 2022-23. The fund is outside the RTI Act; figures shown here are what the Fund has itself disclosed. Cross-check against the downloadable PDFs above.

Disasters & relief columns: Calamities (yr) = natural disasters recorded for India in EM-DAT (via the IMF Climate Change Indicators database - floods, storms, landslides, extreme temperature, drought, wildfire). EM-DAT counts are calendar-year, so each is shown against the FY whose Apr-Mar span covers that calendar year (e.g. CY 2023 → FY 2023-24). Hover the chip for the year's major events. PM CARES relief? reads None for every year: per the audited accounts, the Fund's disbursals were entirely COVID-related (FY 2020-21 ₹3,976 cr; FY 2021-22 ₹3,716 cr) and thereafter the PM CARES for Children scheme - no disbursement to any natural calamity appears in any audited year. Cyclone and flood relief (Amphan ₹1,000 cr, Tauktae ₹1,000 cr, etc.) was released through the PMNRF and NDRF instead, not PM CARES. See Frontline (Aug 2026) and The Wire/The News Minute (Aug 2026) for the audit coverage.
