India's foreign-exchange reserves, week ended 6 March 2026

India's foreign-exchange reserves fell by US$11,684 million to US$716,810 million in the week ended 6 March 2026, according to RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement released on 13 March 2026. Foreign currency assets fell US$9,880 million to US$563,245 million. Gold fell US$1,613 million (1.2%) to US$130,017 million. SDRs fell US$146 million to US$18,720 million, and the reserve position in the IMF fell US$45 million to US$4,828 million. Since 26 December 2025, the start of this series, total reserves are up US$20,200 million.

Where these figures come from

RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement for the week ended 6 March 2026, released on 13 March 2026. The WSS is dated a week after the week it describes: "as on 6 March 2026" is the observation date used here, and the release date is when RBI published it. Figures are transcribed, not revised - where RBI later revises a number, RBI's own publication is right.
