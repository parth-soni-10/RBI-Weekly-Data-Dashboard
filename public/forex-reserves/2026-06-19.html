India's foreign-exchange reserves, week ended 19 June 2026

India's foreign-exchange reserves rose by US$962 million to US$672,587 million in the week ended 19 June 2026, according to RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement released on 26 June 2026. Foreign currency assets fell US$3,073 million to US$541,217 million. Gold rose US$4,109 million (4.0%) to US$107,930 million. SDRs fell US$52 million to US$18,647 million, and the reserve position in the IMF fell US$22 million to US$4,793 million. Since 26 December 2025, the start of this series, total reserves are down US$24,023 million.

Where these figures come from

RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement for the week ended 19 June 2026, released on 26 June 2026. The WSS is dated a week after the week it describes: "as on 19 June 2026" is the observation date used here, and the release date is when RBI published it. Figures are transcribed, not revised - where RBI later revises a number, RBI's own publication is right.
