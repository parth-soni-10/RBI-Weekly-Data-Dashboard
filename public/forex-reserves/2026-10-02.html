India's foreign-exchange reserves, week ended 2 October 2026

India's foreign-exchange reserves fell by US$12,951 million to US$734,606 million in the week ended 2 October 2026, according to RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement released on 9 October 2026. Foreign currency assets fell US$10,664 million to US$604,747 million. Gold fell US$2,288 million (2.1%) to US$106,413 million. SDRs rose US$14 million to US$18,656 million, and the reserve position in the IMF fell US$15 million to US$4,789 million. Since 26 December 2025, the start of this series, total reserves are up US$37,996 million.

Where these figures come from

RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement for the week ended 2 October 2026, released on 9 October 2026. The WSS is dated a week after the week it describes: "as on 2 October 2026" is the observation date used here, and the release date is when RBI published it. Figures are transcribed, not revised - where RBI later revises a number, RBI's own publication is right.
