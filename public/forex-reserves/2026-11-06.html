India's foreign-exchange reserves, week ended 6 November 2026

India's foreign-exchange reserves rose by US$2,491 million to US$719,547 million in the week ended 6 November 2026, according to RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement released on 13 November 2026. Foreign currency assets rose US$232 million to US$591,350 million. Gold rose US$2,205 million (2.1%) to US$104,796 million. SDRs rose US$52 million to US$18,610 million, and the reserve position in the IMF rose US$1 million to US$4,791 million. Since 26 December 2025, the start of this series, total reserves are up US$22,937 million.

Where these figures come from

RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement for the week ended 6 November 2026, released on 13 November 2026. The WSS is dated a week after the week it describes: "as on 6 November 2026" is the observation date used here, and the release date is when RBI published it. Figures are transcribed, not revised - where RBI later revises a number, RBI's own publication is right.
