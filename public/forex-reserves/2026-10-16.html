India's foreign-exchange reserves, week ended 16 October 2026

India's foreign-exchange reserves fell by US$5,011 million to US$721,016 million in the week ended 16 October 2026, according to RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement released on 23 October 2026. Foreign currency assets fell US$5,758 million to US$591,847 million. Gold rose US$706 million (0.7%) to US$105,800 million. SDRs rose US$30 million to US$18,578 million, and the reserve position in the IMF rose US$11 million to US$4,791 million.

Where these figures come from

RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement for the week ended 16 October 2026, released on 23 October 2026. The WSS is dated a week after the week it describes: "as on 16 October 2026" is the observation date used here, and the release date is when RBI published it. Figures are transcribed, not revised - where RBI later revises a number, RBI's own publication is right.
