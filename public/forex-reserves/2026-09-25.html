India's foreign-exchange reserves, week ended 25 September 2026

India's foreign-exchange reserves fell by US$18,344 million to US$747,557 million in the week ended 25 September 2026, according to RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement released on 2 October 2026. Foreign currency assets fell US$15,569 million to US$615,411 million. Gold fell US$2,591 million (2.3%) to US$108,701 million. SDRs fell US$97 million to US$18,642 million, and the reserve position in the IMF fell US$86 million to US$4,804 million.

Where these figures come from

RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement for the week ended 25 September 2026, released on 2 October 2026. The WSS is dated a week after the week it describes: "as on 25 September 2026" is the observation date used here, and the release date is when RBI published it. Figures are transcribed, not revised - where RBI later revises a number, RBI's own publication is right.
