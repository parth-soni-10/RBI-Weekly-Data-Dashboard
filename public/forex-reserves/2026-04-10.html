India's foreign-exchange reserves, week ended 10 April 2026

India's foreign-exchange reserves rose by US$3,825 million to US$700,946 million in the week ended 10 April 2026, according to RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement released on 17 April 2026. Foreign currency assets rose US$3,127 million to US$555,983 million. Gold rose US$601 million (0.5%) to US$121,343 million. SDRs rose US$56 million to US$18,763 million, and the reserve position in the IMF rose US$41 million to US$4,857 million. Since 26 December 2025, the start of this series, total reserves are up US$4,336 million.

Where these figures come from

RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement for the week ended 10 April 2026, released on 17 April 2026. The WSS is dated a week after the week it describes: "as on 10 April 2026" is the observation date used here, and the release date is when RBI published it. Figures are transcribed, not revised - where RBI later revises a number, RBI's own publication is right.
