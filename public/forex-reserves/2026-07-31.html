India's foreign-exchange reserves, week ended 31 July 2026

India's foreign-exchange reserves rose by US$10,512 million to US$692,866 million in the week ended 31 July 2026, according to RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement released on 7 August 2026. Foreign currency assets rose US$8,751 million to US$564,680 million. Gold rose US$1,685 million (1.6%) to US$104,743 million. SDRs rose US$49 million to US$18,666 million, and the reserve position in the IMF rose US$28 million to US$4,778 million. Since 26 December 2025, the start of this series, total reserves are down US$3,744 million.

Where these figures come from

RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement for the week ended 31 July 2026, released on 7 August 2026. The WSS is dated a week after the week it describes: "as on 31 July 2026" is the observation date used here, and the release date is when RBI published it. Figures are transcribed, not revised - where RBI later revises a number, RBI's own publication is right.
