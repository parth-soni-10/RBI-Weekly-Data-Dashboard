India's foreign-exchange reserves, week ended 13 March 2026

India's foreign-exchange reserves fell by US$7,051 million to US$709,759 million in the week ended 13 March 2026, according to RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement released on 20 March 2026. Foreign currency assets fell US$7,677 million to US$555,568 million. Gold rose US$664 million (0.5%) to US$130,681 million. SDRs fell US$23 million to US$18,697 million, and the reserve position in the IMF fell US$14 million to US$4,814 million. Since 26 December 2025, the start of this series, total reserves are up US$13,149 million.

Where these figures come from

RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement for the week ended 13 March 2026, released on 20 March 2026. The WSS is dated a week after the week it describes: "as on 13 March 2026" is the observation date used here, and the release date is when RBI published it. Figures are transcribed, not revised - where RBI later revises a number, RBI's own publication is right.
